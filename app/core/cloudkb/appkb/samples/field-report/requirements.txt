A field engineer can sign in and see the list of sites assigned to them.
An engineer can create an inspection report for a site by filling in a checklist and attaching photos taken on site.
A report saved as a draft on site can be completed and submitted later.
After a report is submitted, its photos are processed into thumbnails and a PDF summary of the report is generated.
A site manager can browse reports by site and date, view the photos, and download the PDF summary.
A manager can mark a report as requiring follow-up, and the engineer sees the follow-up request the next time they sign in.
An administrator manages engineer accounts and site assignments.
Every night the day's reports and photos are backed up.
Inspection records must be kept for five years, and must survive any redeployment or replacement of the service.
Most reports are submitted during working hours on weekdays; almost none arrive at night or on weekends.
Engineers work at outdoor sites and reach the service over the mobile network.
Browsing the report list of a site must respond within 3 seconds.
A failure in photo processing must not prevent engineers from submitting new reports.
