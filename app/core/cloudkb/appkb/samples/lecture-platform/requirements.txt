A student can sign up and log in with an email address and a password.
An instructor can upload a video file for a lecture.
An uploaded video is automatically converted into the format used for playback.
A student can search lectures and view the details of one.
A student can pay for a lecture and enrol in it. Payment is handled through an external payment provider.
A student's playback position is stored so that the next visit resumes where they left off.
An instructor can see the number of enrolled students and the revenue for each of their lectures.
An administrator can take an inappropriate lecture out of public view.
Listing lectures must respond within 2 seconds.
In the first week of a semester the traffic is about ten times the usual level.
Card details are never stored by us; they are handed to the payment provider.
Student personal data must be kept inside the country.
New features must be released without taking the service down.
If video conversion fails, the rest of the service must keep working for students.
